## Lunch Time: A Precious Memory

My wife and I had lunch at a restaurant that holds a special place in our hearts — it was the very spot where we first 
met. As I remember, We have dined there together three times so far.  It has been nearly four years since I met my 
wife, and we got married in 2024. Our encounter was through a blind date.

I still remember clearly that I dressed in a formal suit for that first meeting. She was running late because of work, 
so I waited for her for more than 30 minutes. We ordered a spicy fish dish for lunch. During the meal, we chatted 
awkwardly with no specific topics. It was typical of a blind date; we were strangers to each other and both a little shy 
and nervous.

After lunch, I went to her shop, Lazy Cat, which sells coffee and soft drinks like lemon tea. I guess the shop got its 
name because she loves cats and has a gentle, easy-going personality. I stayed there for the rest of the afternoon, 
keeping her company and helping her out occasionally. 
I come to find her about each 2 week since I lived in HK. After serval months, she became my girlfriend. Ha ha.
